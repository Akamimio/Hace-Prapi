# Hace Prapi Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 7, 2026

Hace Prapi does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The game does not require registration and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. Your best score may be stored locally on your device and is not sent to a server. You can remove this data by clearing the application's site data or uninstalling the application. The game can run offline after its files have been cached.

For privacy questions, contact the publisher using the contact information provided in the Microsoft Store listing.
